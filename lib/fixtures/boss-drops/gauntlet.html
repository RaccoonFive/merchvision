The reward chest can be found in the lobby of The Gauntlet. After attempting The Gauntlet, players can loot the chest to find rewards for their efforts. If your inventory is full when attempting to loot the reward chest, you will receive the chat message: "You need a free inventory space for whatever you may find in the chest." If you have a space free and you receive multiple items, the extra items will be dropped on the ground.
